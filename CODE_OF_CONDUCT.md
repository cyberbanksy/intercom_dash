# Code of conduct

This applies to this repository and to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
project space. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the next person who reads them.
- Respect people's time: search before filing, and include what's needed to reproduce a problem.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report problems to **support@orgista.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity
is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what is a violation and may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they find
inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Decisions follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
